# Real-user validation kit

**Goal:** find out whether two real founders or product managers would use this to make better product decisions, and which outputs they don't trust.

## Who to recruit (2 people minimum)
- One product manager at a consumer app (food, quick commerce, fintech, mobility) who reads app reviews at least monthly.
- One founder or early product hire at a startup who handles customer feedback themselves.
- Ask through LinkedIn, college alumni, or product communities. Offer to share what you learn with them.

**Message template**
> Hi {name}, I'm a final-year student building a Voice of Customer tool. It turns messy app reviews and social posts into ranked, evidence-backed product problems. Could I have 30 minutes to watch you use the demo on a Zomato practice dataset? I'm looking for where it's wrong or confusing, not for praise. Happy to share the findings with you.

## Session plan (30 minutes, screen shared, recorded with permission)
1. **Context (3 min).** "How do you deal with customer feedback today? When did it last change a decision?"
2. **Cold task (7 min).** Give no tour. "You're the Zomato product manager. Using this, decide the one problem your team should work on next quarter." Watch. Note where they click first and where they hesitate.
3. **Trust probe (7 min).** Open the Investigation page for their chosen problem. "Would you repeat this number in a leadership meeting? What would you check first?"
4. **Decision probe (5 min).** In the Decision Engine, ask them to approve or reject two items and fill in effort.
5. **The five questions (6 min).**
   - Does it help you prioritise better? Compared with what you do today?
   - Are the insights useful? Which one most?
   - Which outputs do you **not** trust? Why?
   - What confused you?
   - What would you improve or remove?
6. **Close (2 min).** "Would you use this weekly if it ran on your real data? What would have to be true?"

## What to record (one row per observation)

| Participant | Page | What they did or said (verbatim if possible) | Type (trust / confusion / value / request) | Severity 1-3 | Change made |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Scoring after each session
- Task success: did they pick a problem and justify it with evidence from the tool? (yes / partly / no)
- Trust: "I'd repeat these numbers to my manager" on a 1-5 scale
- Usefulness: "This would change what my team works on" on a 1-5 scale
- Time to a decision, in minutes
